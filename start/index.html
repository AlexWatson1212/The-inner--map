What is happening?

01

My capacity changes without warning

Map the load, recovery, support and timing around a moment when your ability changed.

Useful for inconsistent functioning, overload or ‘why could I do it yesterday?’

02

I want to begin, but cannot make myself start

Look at clarity, size, interest, transitions, pressure and the first visible step.

Useful for task initiation, avoidance, freezing or getting stuck between tasks.

03

I feel overwhelmed and do not know what I need

Separate sensory input, emotional demand, uncertainty and bodily or practical needs.

Useful when ‘too much’ is accurate but not yet specific enough to act on.

Map this moment →
04

I have stopped, but I am not recovering

Notice whether your version of rest reduces input, responsibility and internal pressure.

Useful when downtime remains agitating, numbing, guilty or surprisingly effortful.

Map this moment →
05

I want connection, but interaction feels too demanding

Map the type, timing, intensity and recovery cost of contact—not only whether you want it.

Useful for conflicting needs, messages, calls, plans and post-social recovery.

Map this moment →
06

I have adapted for so long that I do not know what fits

Notice which choices bring steadiness, energy or relief when nobody is grading the result.

Useful for masking, inherited expectations, identity and life-design questions.
