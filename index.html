The mapping process

Observe first. Explain carefully. Change one thing.

The process is deliberately non-linear. Return to whichever step is useful; stop when continuing would cost more than it gives.

See the full approach
  1. 01

    Pause

    Check whether you have enough capacity to reflect right now.

  2. 02

    Notice

    Capture one specific moment and the conditions around it.

  3. 03

    Make sense

    Hold more than one possible explanation; separate evidence from interpretation.

  4. 04

    Test

    Choose one small, reversible change that reduces cost or adds support.

  5. 05

    Review

    Ask what changed, what it cost and what deserves to be kept.

A field guide, not a rulebook

The map should become more like you over time.

Neurodivergent people are not all the same. Context, health, history, responsibilities and resources all affect what is possible.

01

Patterns before labels

A label may provide context. Your repeated, specific examples show what needs attention.

02

Capacity before judgement

Ask what load, recovery, uncertainty or support surrounded the moment before judging it.

03

Experiments before rules

Try one small, reversible change. Keep what helps; revise what does not.

04

A life before performance

The aim is not endless output. It is a life with enough meaning, connection and room to recover.
