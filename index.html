Open the 30-second version

One question, four options, and no follow-up. Not all of these are possible in every situation, and choosing none of them is a legitimate answer.

  • +Reduce one demand
  • +Reduce one input
  • +Ask for one support
  • +Stop choosing for now
  • If none of these fit and things feel unmanageable rather than heavy, that is a real situation and not a failure of effort. In England: NHS 111 online or 111 (mental health option), 999 if there is immediate danger to life, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time.

The mapping process

Observe first. Explain carefully. Change one thing.

The process is deliberately non-linear. Return to whichever step is useful, and stop when continuing would cost more than it gives.

    Notice

    Capture one specific moment and the conditions around it.

  3. 03

    Make sense

    Hold more than one possible explanation; keep evidence and interpretation separate.

  4. 04

    Test

    Choose one small, reversible change that may reduce cost or add support.

  5. 05

    Review

    Ask what changed, what it cost and what deserves to be kept.

A field guide, not a rulebook

The map should become more like you over time.

Neurodivergent people are not all the same. Context, health, history, responsibilities, money and support all affect what is possible—and neurodivergence will not be the explanation for everything that is hard.

01

Patterns before labels

A label may provide context. Your repeated, specific examples are what show where attention is needed.

02

Capacity before judgement

Ask what load, recovery, uncertainty or support surrounded the moment before judging it.

03

Experiments before rules

Try one small, reversible change. Keep what seems to help; revise what does not.

04

A life before performance

The aim is not endless output. It is a life with enough meaning, connection and room to recover.

Clear boundaries

Useful without pretending to be clinical care.

The Inner Map is educational, reflective and evidence-aware. It does not diagnose, provide therapy, replace medical care, or tell you that one explanation must be true. Nothing here can show whether you are autistic, ADHD or AuDHD.

If you need urgent help with your mental health in England, contact NHS 111 online or call 111 and select the mental health option. If someone’s life is at risk, call 999 or go to A&E.
