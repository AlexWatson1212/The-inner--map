What this is not

      
A draft you can abandon is doing its job.

    

    

      
This is not a task manager, a schedule or a habit system. It exists to reduce the thinking
        required to begin a day—so it should cost less than it gives, and it should be easy to
        walk away from.

      
If the shape you made does not fit by lunchtime, the shape was a hypothesis and the day is
        the evidence. Changing it is the point.
